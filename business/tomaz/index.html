我们的故事

TOMAZ started with a small shoe kiosk at Amcorp Mall. From the beginning, the brand focused on making well-designed products more accessible to Malaysian customers. As the business grew, TOMAZ expanded beyond footwear into additional categories including bags, clothing, watches, accessories and gaming furniture. The brand continues to develop products for customers who value practical design, comfort and accessible pricing.
